## Projects

### ⚖️ [Verdict](https://github.com/Fibinachi/Verdict) — Agentic Jury Modelling
A courtroom simulator where AI agents (jurors, judge, lawyers, witnesses) form opinions from trial events, evidence, and testimony.
- Each juror carries a persistent **memory file** encoding cultural priors (news diet, demographics, life experience) that conditions how new evidence is interpreted — path-dependent, belief-updating jurors
- Memories carry **strength tags by type**: narrative testimony persists, dry exhibits (spreadsheets) decay — reproducing the bias structure of real juries, not just verdicts
- Full .NET 9 WPF app: 12-seat jury box, multi-LLM providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok, Ollama), transcript processing, evidence admission, bias weight calibration, PDF reports

### 🗺️ GRID — Global Religious Infrastructure Database
The world's largest open structured dataset of places of worship: **4.9 million religious sites, 244 countries, 96.7% geocoded, 311 denominations/traditions** — built solo.
- A civil-infrastructure layer for maps: religious sites anchor emergency planning, demographic analysis, and location intelligence the way fire stations and hospitals do
- Enrichment: websites, phones, emails, diocesan/census/demographic metadata, cross-source corroboration (OSM, Overture, Foursquare, IRS/EIN, CRA, Wikidata, national registries)
- Demos: [interactive Chicago map](https://github.com/Fibinachi/grid-demos) · [build pipeline](https://github.com/Fibinachi/grid-demos-v2)
